    Disclaimer

    Please read this

    This lab is a workshop and education project. Maxime and Hagrid are simulated from wiring diagrams plus simplified neuron rules — there are no real flies in this computer, and nothing here is scientific or medical advice. Neuron counts and behaviours are illustrative of the underlying datasets; where the sim cannot test something (taste, vision, learning, wind, CO₂), it says so on the relevant page.

    Data use follows the FlyWire Terms. Accounts on the lab server store salted password hashes, never readable passwords; demo-mode accounts never leave your browser. The server is workshop-grade — do not reuse an important password here.
